# Use of language models in writing the article

**English** · [Português (Brasil)](language-model-use.pt-BR.md)

Language models supported the search, the code, the drafting, the review, and the double reading of speeches (Appendix J). This version was also written by language models: Gemini 4 Argon for sections 1 and 2 and Claude Opus 5.5 for the rest, under written rules that prohibit any number or citation outside the verified material. Three models (GPT-6 Astra, Gemini 4 Argon, and Grok 4.7) reviewed the entire article, without seeing one another's reports in the first round. Models also took part in coding the speeches and in checking the 160 GGB cases, with a second coder from another model family for some of the cases (section 5). In the GGB check, 23 cases were double-coded, with 21 valid pairs: agreement between the two coders, discounting chance (κ, where 1 is perfect agreement), was high for the victim's LGBT+ status (κ = .91) and moderate for the confirmation of violent death (κ = .46), which is therefore only indicative under the rule fixed beforehand (Appendices B and F). In the 6 cases with disagreement, a third coder from another model family, blind to the first two, broke the ties between the readings; only one case changed, and the 50 verifiable cases did not change. The numbers come from programs run on the public databases or from documents saved in the study's repository, and works read only in abstract form are marked in the text. Source checking, method, and authorship rest with the author alone. This translation from the Brazilian Portuguese original was produced by Claude Sonnet 5.5 and reviewed sentence by sentence by Claude Opus 5.5; a program checked that every number, link and table matches the original. The Portuguese text is the version of record.
